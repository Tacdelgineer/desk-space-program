ORDER OF A SHORT (20-40 s)
  0-1 s       hook: the most surprising moment + big claim or question text
  ~2 s        00 intro clip + 01 intro title (on a stamp sound or a music hit)
  2-3 s       04 guess card (optional). Hold it long enough to read
  middle      the test: one control, one change. 02 corner badge + captions
  last 3-4 s  03 mission report. Its NEXT MISSION line is the outro.
              End on a frame that loops back to the hook

TEXT STYLES
  Hook + captions    Anton, cream, black stroke, one key word in yellow
  Mission number     JetBrains Mono ExtraBold, red (BLANK title only)
  Mission name       Anton, cream, inside the black strip, max ~14 characters
  Guess options      Anton, ink
  Report subtitle    JetBrains Mono ExtraBold, ink: mission + model,
                     e.g. "M02 GEMINI   QWEN 32B, 4-BIT"
  Report values      Anton, ink. A machine that doesn't fit: NO in red
  The lesson         Anton, ink, one line
  Next mission       Anton, cream
  Colours            cream #EFE5CF   ink #1B1712   red #DF3A2C
                     yellow #F2C230  teal #1F8783 (Spark column)

MISSION REPORT
  Only numbers from the lab's data file. Add "est." until the Spark is measured.
  FITS? = YES or NO.  TOKENS/S = writing speed.  DONE IN = time to finish the answer.
  Columns stay in the same order every episode: DGX Spark, RTX 5090, Mac Studio,
  and from episode 9 on a fourth, Strix Halo (the background image needs that column).
  Add "rep." to the Strix Halo's numbers: they are other people's published runs.

FILMING
  Hook and reveal: phone pointed at the screen.
  Close-ups: OBS recording of the lab with H pressed (keys 2 and 3 for memory and GPU).
  B-roll: C for the slow orbit.   Long waits: 5x playback.
  Music: your own track at about -18 dB.
